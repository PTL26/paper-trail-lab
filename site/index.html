Paper Trail Lab

AI trading tools, tested in public — including the ones that fail.

Paper Trail Lab independently tests AI trading tools and trading ideas. For every test, the question, the data, the rules and the pass/fail gates are written down and published before any result is seen, and the verdict follows those rules: PASS, FAIL or INCONCLUSIVE. Nothing here is investment advice.
