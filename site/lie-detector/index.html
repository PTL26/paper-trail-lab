Your backtest says you beat the market. This test measures how much of that edge is only the market going up while you held.

Runs in your browser. Your trades are not uploaded anywhere.

    What this test cannot see

    It only sees returns. It cannot detect look-ahead bugs in your code, survivorship in your stock universe, or fills you would not have got live. Passing here is necessary. It is not sufficient.

    Matched trades

    Method. Least-squares fit of each trade's return on the benchmark's return over the same holding period. Beta is the slope, alpha is what is left at zero market move. The 95% range comes from 2,000 bootstrap resamples drawn by entry week, so trades that overlap in time stay together. The same input always gives the same result.

    A testing tool, not investment advice.
