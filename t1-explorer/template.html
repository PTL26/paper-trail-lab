What if entry had required a positive spread?

The pre-registered rule bought every eligible event the day after the announcement, even when the market price was already above the offer — a design error in the study's brief. Below: the same events, keeping only those where the entry-day price was below the offer price (for Dutch auctions, below the low end of the range in the strict version, below the high end in the lenient one).

Method, briefly

Data. Tender-offer, going-private and proxy filings from SEC EDGAR, grouped into events. A language model read the filings into fields (offer type, odd-lot priority, cash-out terms, outcome, final price); every value cites its source filing, and a sample was checked by a second model and partly by hand.

Trade rule. Buy 99 shares at the close of the first trading day after the announcement; tender; collect the final price paid. Costs: $1 per order, $2 currency conversion per event. Results in euros.

What is published. Derived results only — net result and percentage per gate event, bucket, reason, outcome — plus offer terms from SEC filings. No market price data is reproduced.
